Analysis framework: hero/first 5 seconds, navigation & IA, case-study count & order, structure, impact/metrics, solo/0→1 signal, visual craft, B2B credibility.

### 1. Meghna — Striim Automated Pipelines

**Best overall model for this portfolio.**

| Dimension | Pattern |
|-----------|---------|
| **Hero / first 5 sec** | Business crisis hook: "$925k lost revenue, 6,000 support tickets" — not "I design delightful experiences" |
| **Nav & IA** | Single long case study, sticky section nav, "Up Next" at bottom |
| **Case study count** | Deep on 1 flagship; other work linked from nav |
| **Structure** | Context → Crisis → Evolving role (V1/V2/V3) → Research → Three iterations → Failure → Integration → Learnings |
| **Impact** | Hard numbers everywhere; also honest about V2 generating zero revenue |
| **Solo/0→1** | "V1: Sole designer" explicitly; design system as headline contribution |
| **Visual craft** | Screens + quotes + test excerpts; not poster-scroll |
| **B2B credibility** | Customer quote as loss signal; competitive analysis (Qlik, Fivetran, Airbyte) |

**Red flag avoided:** Doesn't hide the product strategy failure — that *increases* seniority signal.

## Pattern Checklist

Watch for these as you browse — they're where portfolios win or lose.

### Hero (first 5 seconds)

**Winners lead with:**
- Specific domain + outcome: *"B2B SaaS · sole designer · AI call QA platform"*
- A number or stakes: *"$925k lost revenue"* or *"+475% homepage key events"*
- Not: *"I craft delightful, human-centered experiences"*

### Case-study count & order

| Pattern | Who uses it |
|---------|-------------|
| **3 strong, 1 featured** | Most hire-ready portfolios (NN/g, UXMagic, Portfolio Studio) |
| **Featured = hardest/most senior problem** | Meghna, Eugene, Daniel Autry |
| **Secondary = different skill proof** | Homepage/conversion, design system, 0→1 mobile |

**Recommended order for this portfolio:** Gistly (featured) → Homepage redesign → Serenity.

### Case-study structure (the seniority signal)

Consensus structure across IxDF, NN/g, Medium teardowns, and top portfolios:

```
1. Hook / Executive summary (role + outcome upfront)
2. Context (company, users, your scope)
3. Problem (diagnosis with data/quotes — not adjectives)
4. Constraints (NDA, timeline, team, tech, compliance)
5. Decisions with tradeoffs ← THE SENIORITY SIGNAL
6. Process evidence (flows, tests, iterations — supporting, not leading)
7. Outcome (metrics + honest attribution)
8. Reflection (what failed, what you'd do differently)
```

**The #1 failure mode:** Behance-style poster scrolls that show *what* but hide *why* and *what it cost to decide*.

### Impact & metrics

| When you have numbers | When you don't |
|----------------------|----------------|
| Lead with them in the hook | Use qualitative proof: user quotes, heatmaps, support ticket themes |
| Show baseline → lift | Name the measurement method (GA4, Clarity, usability test N=8) |
| Attribute honestly | e.g. "Homepage redesign; traffic growth kept separate" |

**Business impact isn't always revenue:**
- Time-to-setup (hours → minutes)
- Support ticket reduction
- Task completion / error rate
- Adoption of a feature
- Deal velocity / demo conversion

### Scope line (solo designer quantification)

Best examples literally count breadth:

> "Sole designer: customer app, admin panel, and design system — 10 months, pre-MVP to launch" — Chistiakov

> "V1 sole designer → V2 two-person team → V3 solo again" — Meghna

> "Shaped analytical, forecasting, agentic, and conversational capabilities" — Eugene

**Draft for Gistly:**

> "Sole designer across call auditing, CRM workflows, AI score interpretation, and marketing site — 2.5 years, pre-PMF to production."

### Length & scannability

- **Recruiter scan time:** 55 sec – 3 min (UXMagic 2026)
- **Case study read time:** Under 5 min for first pass; depth available on scroll
- **Scannable markers:** Section labels, role/timeframe/tools pills, pull quotes, numbered decisions
- **One idea per section** — short paragraphs, not walls of text

### Proof of shipping

| Strong | Weak |
|--------|------|
| Live product links (gistly.ai) | Figma-only frames |
| Real UI in context (dashboards, not isolated screens) | Dribbble-style mockups |
| Before/after or iteration sequences | Single polished final state |
| Motion/screen recordings for complex flows | Static poster layouts |

## Common Patterns Across Strong B2B Portfolios

1. **Problem-first, not process-first** — crisis or business gap before any wireframes
2. **3 projects max on homepage** — depth beats breadth
3. **Metrics in the teaser, not buried** — Eugene and the homepage card both do this
4. **Named tradeoffs** — "We chose X over Y because…" not double-diamond diagrams
5. **Solo scope is a feature** — quantify areas, screens, systems owned
6. **Honest failure increases trust** — Meghna's V2 flop, Chistiakov's testing issues
7. **Founder/PM/stakeholder quotes** — third-party validation
8. **Design system as proof of systems thinking** — woven into flagship case study, not a separate side project
9. **Live links + real product shots** — signals shipping, not concepts
10. **Scannable structure** — numbered sections, executive summary, section nav

## Differentiators of the Best Portfolios

| Portfolio | What sets it apart |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Meghna** | Willingness to show strategic failure; evolving role over years |
| **Simon Pan** | Reframes the problem from field research; ties to $1M/week ops cost |
| **Eugene** | Every project card = one metric; founder testimonials |
| **Chistiakov** | Hypothesis-driven testing (31 hypotheses, 9 critical issues) |
| **Daniel Autry** | Restraint — 2–3 projects, each a complete story |

The best aren't the flashiest. They're the ones where a hiring manager can answer in 60 seconds: *What problem? What did they decide? What changed?*

## Five Concrete Things to Copy

### 1. Crisis hook at top of Gistly case study

Mirror Meghna: lead with the business pain before the solution.

**Draft:**

> "Sales teams couldn't trust AI call scores — auditors were ignoring the product."

### 2. Scope quantification block

Copy Chistiakov's opening backtick line:

> "Sole designer: call ingestion → AI scoring → audit workflows → CRM integration — 2.5 yrs, 0→1 to production."

### 3. Named decisions section (3–4 tradeoffs)

From the Medium teardown: don't list "research → ideate → prototype." Instead:

> "Chose explainability over score simplicity because non-engineer auditors were the primary users."

### 4. Metrics with attribution honesty

Already done on the homepage case study (+475% with GA4/Clarity attribution). Apply the same pattern to Gistly: baseline → method → lift → what you can't claim.

### 5. Reflection / failure section

Meghna's "Design success ≠ Product success" is the most memorable part of her portfolio. Even one paragraph on what you'd do differently at Gistly (e.g. earlier design system investment, different IA bet) signals seniority.
